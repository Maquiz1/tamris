CONDITIONS OF LISTING

  1. The medicine shall comply with all relevant provisions of the Traditional and Alternative Medicine Council, Cap 244 and regulations made thereunder at all times.
  2. The listing holder shall ensure that the medicine complies with the details approved by the Council at all times.
  3. The listing holder shall ensure that the medicine complies with Tanzanian labelling requirements at all times.
  4. The listing holder shall ensure that the medicine is prepared in a clean and suitable place, as described in the application.
  5. The listing holder and any supplier shall ensure that the medicine is stored and transported in accordance with the instructions on this certificate.
  6. The listing holder shall apply for renewal of the listing 90 days before its expiry.
  7. The Council reserves the right to withdraw this certificate when conditions 1 to 6 are contravened, or when the risks of the medicine outweigh its benefits, or when it is in the public interest to do so.
  8. The listing holder shall report any adverse effects of the medicine to the Council.
