CONDITIONS OF REGISTRATION

  1. The medicine shall comply with all relevant provisions of the Traditional and Alternative Medicine Council, Cap 244 and regulations made there under at all times.
  2. The marketing authorization holder shall ensure that the medicines comply with specifications approved by the Council at all times.
  3. The marketing authorization holder shall ensure that the medicines comply with Tanzanian labeling requirements at all times.
  4. The marketing authorization holder shall ensure that the manufacturing facilities where a registered medicine is produced comply at all times with minimum Tanzanian Good Manufacturing Practices requirements.
  5. The marketing authorization holder, Manufacturer and supplier shall ensure that medicines within their control are stored and transported in accordance with the instructions and information provided in this certificate.
  6. The marketing authorization holder shall ensure that application for renewal of registration is made 90 days before expiry of registration.
  7. The registration of the medicines shall continue to be valid provided that annual retention fee is paid annually before 31st of January each year.
  8. The Council reserves the right to withdrawal this certificate when condition 1 to 7 are contravened and when the risks of the medicine outweighs the benefits or it is in public interest to do so.
  9. The marketing authorization holder is duty bound to conduct periodic post-marketing surveillance and safety studies of registered medicines and report the outcome of such studies to the Council.
